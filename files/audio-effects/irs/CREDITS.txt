kemar_stereo_30deg.irs - true-stereo binaural impulse response (EasyEffects convolver)

Derived from the MIT Media Lab KEMAR HRTF measurements, diffuse-field equalized set
(https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html, file diffuse.zip, member elev0/H0e030a.wav,
sha256 of diffuse.zip a48e73137390626ef1e45d1de6a4d98530c6aa570e71b40f7c4442241ed5fa22).

    Bill Gardner and Keith Martin, "HRTF Measurements of a KEMAR Dummy-Head Microphone",
    MIT Media Lab Perceptual Computing Technical Report #280, 1994.

Licence (from the archive README): "This data is Copyright 1994 by the MIT Media Laboratory.
It is provided free with no restrictions on use, provided the authors are cited when the data
is used in any research or commercial application."

Processing (tools/make_hrir.py): +-30 deg azimuth, 0 deg elevation, mirrored for the left
speaker; 16 samples of leading silence; windowed-sinc resampling 44.1 -> 48 kHz; peak -1 dBFS;
4-channel 16-bit PCM WAV, channels LL, LR, RL, RR.
